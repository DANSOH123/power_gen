About Conventional Power Gen Standards

Designed & Developed by Dan Soh

Conventional Power Gen Standards is an interactive technical reference tool engineered to bridge the complexity of international engineering standards across thermal, combined-cycle, and conventional power generation facilities.

Mission

Navigating through hundreds of individual standards (ASME, API, IEEE, IEC, ISO, NFPA, ASTM) to establish cross-disciplinary linkages can be overwhelming. This platform synthesizes complex standard hierarchies into a clear, interactive visual format.

Key Features

⚡ Domain Mapping: Grouped by Mechanical (ASME), Rotating Equipment (API), Electrical (IEEE/IEC), Systems (ISO), and Safety (NFPA/ASTM).

🔗 Lifecycle Hierarchy: Visualizing standards along the engineering lifecycle — Design → Fabrication → Installation → Testing → Operations.

🔍 Instant Search: Quick lookup for standard codes, purpose summaries, and cross-linked dependencies.
